About

Philadelphia, PA

I'm a junior at Penn studying Mechanical Engineering with a concentration in Dynamics, Controls, and Robotics, and finishing a Master's in Robotics at the same time through Penn's accelerated program. Both degrees are conferred in May 2028.

Most of what I've built comes back to the same question: how do you design something that actually works for the person using it? At Penn ADAPT that meant redesigning a bike adapter for cyclists with prosthetic limbs after a screw-stripping failure was costing riders more than a thousand dollars a year, and later leading a project with the Philadelphia Adaptive Sports Center on adaptive shoelaces so athletes with physical disabilities could get dressed without help.

Last summer I moved from hardware into clinical software at One Health Partners. I shadowed physicians for more than fifty hours before writing a line of code, then co-built an iPhone app that measures wounds with LiDAR and drafts the clinical note, and an EMR system that turns a physician's notes into audio a patient can actually understand.

I'm also a walk-on Division I track athlete and an Ivy League championship scorer, training twenty-plus hours a week. Track is where I learned most of what I know about iteration: you find the gap between where something is and where it needs to be, and then you close it by showing up again.

I'm looking for Summer 2027 internships in robotics, controls, and mechanical design.
